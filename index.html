Not Pumpkins!

The first Jack-o'-lanterns were not made from pumpkins. People in Ireland carved scary faces into turnips (a white root vegetable). Pumpkins were only used later in America!

The Good Spider

Are you scared of spiders? An old myth says that if you see a spider on Halloween, it is not a monster. It is the spirit of a dead family member watching over you.

"Witch" means Smart

The English word "witch" comes from an old word wicce. A long time ago, this word did not mean a scary lady. It meant a "wise woman" who knew about medicine and nature.

FILE: A Tale of Two Holidays

>> GLOSSARY (Slovníček)

  • Grave: hrob

  • Costume: kostým

Welcome to the archive. Let's compare two different traditions: Halloween and Dušičky.

Halloween is celebrated on October 31st. It started more than 2,000 years ago with the Celtic people in Europe. They called this festival 'Samhain'. They believed that on this night, the door between the living world and the dead world was open. Ghosts could walk on Earth! Because people were afraid, they wore scary masks and costumes to hide from the ghosts.

Today, teenagers in the USA and UK love Halloween. They go to loud parties, watch horror movies, and wear cool costumes to get candy.

In the Czech Republic, you have Dušičky (All Souls' Day) on November 2nd. It is very different from Halloween. Dušičky is a quiet and respectful holiday. Families visit the cemetery. They clean the graves, put flowers there, and light candles. They do this to remember family members who died.

Halloween is loud, fast, and scary. Dušičky is quiet, slow, and sad. But remember this interesting fact: both holidays started for the same reason – to think about the dead.
